Everyone unwraps their own bar

Swipe

Rip the paper

Then the foil

One more…

Eight stop-motion steps: paper, foil, then what’s inside.

The finger drives it. The phone only learns what’s in the bar at the very last step.

This is the participant’s moment. The bar fills the phone; swiping tears it open one frame at a time. Nobody can peek: the server only reveals a bar’s contents once it is fully open.
