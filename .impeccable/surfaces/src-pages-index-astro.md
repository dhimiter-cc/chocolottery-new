## Scope
Homepage (`src/pages/index.astro`), Persuade mode, plus the app-wide theme it establishes (leaderboard, game room shell). Everyone lands here: start a round in one move, then browse what happened in past rounds. No join-by-code form any more (players arrive by QR/link). Primary action reads "Start the event" on EVENT_DATE, "Start a round" otherwise.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The app is a wrapped chocolate bar; you open it to play. Refuses the gradient party-game hero with cards.
OWN-WORLD: Drenched aubergine wrapper paper, flat gold foil blocking (rationed to the wordmark, the tear strip and winners), crinkled silver foil at the wrapper's ends, cream wrapper-back panels, cocoa ink, inkjet dot-matrix lot codes. Extended heavy grotesque, tiny tracked packaging labels.
STORY: A colleague sees the bar, pulls the tear strip, a round opens; scrolling shows the back of the wrapper: a nutrition-facts panel of stats and recent rounds as stamped lot bands.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The viewport is the bar: foil crimps top and bottom, the enormous gold wordmark across the band, packaging micro-copy around it, a full-width perforated gold tear strip ("Pull to start a round") as the only action; corner labels for leaderboard, fairness, ticket.
FORM: The Wrapper, candidate 3 of 7; seed 10c15add. Signature: the tear strip rips open in stop-motion frames.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
